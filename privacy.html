SELAHVOIX FOR MAC

Privacy

How the app, support email and this documentation site handle information.

About this policy

This policy covers SELAHVOIX for Mac 1.0 and its support website. Publisher and privacy contact: Kenny Renaud, support@selahvoix.com.

Effective 7 November 2026.

Presentations and local storage

SELAHVOIX stores settings, songs, Bibles, themes, recovery data, file-access bookmarks and logs on your Mac. You choose where to save service files. Media may remain external to those files.

The app does not provide a SELAHVOIX online account or automatically upload your presentation library to a publisher-operated server. If you choose a cloud-synced folder or use a backup service, that service operates under its own settings and policies.

Imports and media

PDF and editable PowerPoint-file imports are processed locally. Version 1.0 uses local starter content and manual media imports; it does not include built-in Unsplash or Pixabay searches or automate Microsoft PowerPoint.

The app accesses files you choose and may retain macOS file-access bookmarks for later use. Local logs can contain filenames, paths and technical errors.

Local network control

Phone remote control is off until you start it. Connected clients use a PIN to view service state and control presentation functions on your local network. This connection uses unencrypted HTTP. Enable it only when needed on a trusted network, and stop it when finished.

NDI network video input/output is not included in version 1.0. Existing NDI source settings remain saved and appear as unavailable placeholders.

Email support

If you email support@selahvoix.com, your email address, message and any attachments are sent to the support mailbox. The support mailbox uses Titan Email provided through Hostinger. These providers process email information to provide the service; see Hostinger’s Privacy Policy and Titan’s Privacy Policy. Your own email provider may also process the message.

Kenny Renaud handles SELAHVOIX support emails personally. He does not give anyone else access to the support mailbox or forward copies to anyone else.

Support emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve the issue, unless there is a legal reason to retain them.

Support emails are deleted on request. To request deletion, email support@selahvoix.com.

Send only the information you choose to share. Review logs, screenshots and service files before attaching them.

GitHub Pages hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security when a GitHub Pages site is visited, including visits by people who are not signed in. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

This site includes no analytics scripts, advertising, third-party embeds, contact forms or browser-storage code. Following an external link or sending email takes you to another service with its own privacy practices.

Your choices

You can stop phone remote control and manage your saved services and exported files. Libraries, settings and recovery files can remain separately after you delete a service. macOS permissions, backups and cloud-sync settings also affect access and retention.

For privacy questions, contact Kenny Renaud at support@selahvoix.com. You can request deletion of your support emails at this address.
